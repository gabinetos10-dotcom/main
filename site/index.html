Plate 01 · Manifesto

Overprint everything.

Every studio says it blends design and engineering. We mean it mechanically. A website is a press run: strategy is the imposition, code is the plate, craft is the ink1. Run them separately and you get flat, forgettable color. Run them over each other — deliberately, slightly out of register — and colors appear that none of us owned alone.

That is what GJS sells. Not deliverables in four separate folders, but the overlap: a site that already knows your operations, an automation that respects your brand, data that arrives shaped like a decision2. Six inks, one sheet, no gloss.

Where the layers overlap, something new appears.

Plate 05 · Service — Consulting

Consulting

Before any ink touches paper, someone has to decide what is worth printing. We audit stacks, untangle roadmaps, and tell you — kindly, in writing — which rewrite to cancel. Strategy at GJS is not a slide deck; it is the imposition plan the whole press run follows.

Engagements run two to six weeks: interviews, a technical read of what you actually have, and a route sheet of what to build, buy, automate or delete3. No retainer theatre. You leave with a plan your own team can print from.

3 Consulting has no exhibit on this plate. Its game is hidden behind the misprint — advisors love a secret.
