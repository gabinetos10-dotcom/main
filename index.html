The Atelier

A little house of joy, obsessed with the details others forget.

Maison Jolie began with a single belief: your wedding should feel like the warmest day of your life, poured into a single golden afternoon. We are a small French atelier of planners, stylists and dreamers — and we take on only a handful of couples each season.

We handle the invisible logistics so you can stay drunk on the moment. Then we style everything — from the light on the linen to the last petal on the aisle — so it looks unmistakably, joyfully you.
